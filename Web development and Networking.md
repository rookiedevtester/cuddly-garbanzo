CSMA/CD
A protocol applied to sending messages to devices on the ethernet bus. 
The messages are started sending when none of the signals are detected on the bus. If there is a interruption or one of devices are transmitting a signal, the system is pause for a period before restarting the same process.

CSMA/CA
Same as CSMA/CD except for being used in wireless communication. 
The signal is not transmitted until there is no interruption in the communication in the waiting period.
